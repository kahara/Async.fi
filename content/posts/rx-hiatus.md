After some 500+ days online, the balconic (6m, 2m, 70cm)
[receivers](/posts/2024-12-24-rx-1/) are going offline for an
unspecified amount of time. We're moving to a new apartment in
a neighbouring town, and I'll have to organize things over there
a bit first, before being able to focus on amateur radio-related
matters again.

For future reference, compositions for running the
([luarvique/openwebrx](https://github.com/luarvique/openwebrx))
receivers and a
([caddyserver/caddy](https://github.com/caddyserver/caddy))
web server in front are available at
[kahara/rx-1.async.fi](https://github.com/kahara/rx-1.async.fi/branches).
Don't forget to change
[the](https://github.com/kahara/rx-1.async.fi/blob/6m/openwebrx/var/users.json)
[default](https://github.com/kahara/rx-1.async.fi/blob/2m/openwebrx/var/users.json)
[passwords](https://github.com/kahara/rx-1.async.fi/blob/70cm/openwebrx/var/users.json)
for OpenWebRX+ before going online.
